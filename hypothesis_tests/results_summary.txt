INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  H₀ REJECTED at alpha=0.05. Shannon diversity has a statistically significant POSITIVE association with loss ratio (r_s = +0.1266, p = 2.96e-11).
  Effect size is moderate (|r_s| = 0.1266).
  Mann-Whitney confirms: HIGH-diversity counties have a HIGHER median loss ratio (0.896 vs 0.721, p = 7.02e-12, rb_r = -0.1513).

  NOTE: County-year tests include ~4 repeated observations per county (years 1999–2001, 2004).
  County-average tests (independent observations) are the primary inference basis.
